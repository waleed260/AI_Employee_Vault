# SKILL.md - Generate CEO Briefing

## Purpose
Create a comprehensive Monday Morning CEO Briefing summarizing business performance, tasks, and recommendations.

## Trigger
- Manual: "Generate Briefing" or "Create Weekly Report"
- Automated: Every Sunday at 8:00 PM via cron

## Data Sources

1. `/Accounting/` - Financial transactions
2. `/Done/` - Completed tasks
3. `/Plans/` - Active projects
4. `Business_Goals.md` - Targets and metrics

## Briefing Structure

```markdown
---
generated: {timestamp}
period: {start} to {end}
---

# Monday Morning CEO Briefing

## Executive Summary
{2-3 sentence overview}

## Revenue
- This Week: ${amount}
- MTD: ${amount} ({percentage} of target)
- Trend: {on track/behind/ahead}

## Completed Tasks
- [x] {task 1}
- [x] {task 2}

## Bottlenecks
| Task | Expected | Actual | Delay |
|------|----------|--------|-------|
| {task} | {days} | {days} | +{days} |

## Financial Summary
- Income: ${amount}
- Expenses: ${amount}
- Net: ${amount}

## Proactive Suggestions
### Cost Optimization
- **{Service}**: No usage in {days} days. Cost: ${amount}/mo.
  - [ACTION] Cancel subscription?

### Upcoming Deadlines
- {Project}: {date} ({days} days)
- {Deliverable}: {date} ({days} days)

---
*Generated by AI Employee v0.1*
```

## Success Criteria
- All revenue accurately calculated
- Bottlenecks identified with specific delays
- At least 1 proactive suggestion
- Saved to `/Briefings/{date}_Briefing.md`
